My Story

Crafting Confidence,
One Brushstroke at a Time

With over ten years transforming faces and stories, I've built my artistry at the intersection of technique and emotion. From intimate bridal mornings to high-voltage editorial shoots in London and Paris, every look I create is a conversation between the person in my chair and the best version of themselves.

Trained at the London College of Fashion and certified by MAC Cosmetics, my philosophy is simple: makeup should feel like you — only elevated. I work exclusively with luxury, skin-kind formulas to ensure my art stays flawless from first light to final dance.
